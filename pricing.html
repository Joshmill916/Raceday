How buying works

Pay, paste, race.

There's no account to create and nothing to wait on. You pay through Stripe, your access code appears on the next screen, and you paste it into RaceDay under Admin → License. That's the whole process.

Where does the code go?
Admin → License, in the app. Paste it in, hit Activate, done. Keep the code — you'll want it if you set up another device.
What counts as a race day?
The first time you sign a driver in on a given date. Building lineups, entering results, and exporting never burn a day.
What if a license runs out mid-season?
Lineups, results, points, and exports keep working. Only new sign-ups pause until you top up — you never lose access to your own data.
Can I get a receipt?
Stripe emails one automatically the moment you pay. Need an invoice for a club treasurer? Email me and I'll sort it out.
