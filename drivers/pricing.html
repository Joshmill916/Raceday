How buying works

Bought in the app. Tied to your profile.

Open Driven, go to Card, and tap Get Pro under Upgrade to Pro. Checkout is through Stripe; your card turns Pro as soon as it's paid.

The Go Pro guide
How do I cancel?
Card → Your plan → Manage subscription. Cancel there any time.
What happens if Pro ends?
Your card goes back to the standard look. Your results, stats and public page all stay.
Got a Pro code?
Card → Have a code? Paste it and tap Unlock Pro.
New phone?
Pro belongs to your profile, not your phone. Restore your backup and it comes with you.
