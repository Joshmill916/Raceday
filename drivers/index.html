How it works

Set it up once. Race anywhere.

Build your card

Open Driven and type your name. Add your number, hometown, sponsors and crew whenever you like.

Publish it

One tap puts your card online and gives you a short link code — like CASEY47R.

Sign up with your code

At a RaceDay track's sign-up table, enter your code. Your card shows up on the lineup — no retyping.

Results come back

When the track wraps up the night, your finishes land on your profile and your public page.

Results you can trust

Posted by the track. Never typed in.

Driven doesn't let drivers type their own finishes onto their page. Results come from the track's official RaceDay scoring, so a win on your page is a win.

Your record adds up across every track and class you race: races, wins and podiums — career, by class, and by track.

At the sign-up table

Your code instead of a clipboard.

Tracks running RaceDay have a Driven link code box on their sign-up screen. Type your code once and the track has your card — and every night after, you just tap your name.

No code handy? Show the QR code on Driven's Link page, or have the race director link your card from the lineup.

Straight answers

What drivers ask first.

Do I have to pay?
No. Your profile, link code, results, stats and public page are free for good. Pro is optional.
Does my track have to use RaceDay?
For results to arrive automatically, yes — RaceDay tracks post them. Your card and public page work either way, and you can add results from other apps yourself.
What if I lose my phone?
Download a backup from Settings and keep it safe. Restoring it on a new phone brings back everything.
What's public?
Only what's on your card: name, number, and anything you add like hometown, bio and sponsors. Your email is never shown.
