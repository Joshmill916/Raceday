Get started

Publish your card

Publishing puts your card online so tracks and fans can see it, and gives you a short link code — the one thing you'll bring to the track.

WhoAny driverTime1 minuteWhereYour phone

  1. Publish

    On the Card tab, under Share with tracks, tap Publish my card. You can also tap it on Home.

  2. Get your link code

    Once it's published, the box shows Published · link code with your code — something like CASEY47R. It never changes.

  3. Find it on the Link page

    The Link tab shows your code big, plus a QR code a race director can scan. Screenshot it so it's handy at the track.

  4. Changes update by themselves

    After publishing, any time you Save changes in Settings your online card updates too. Update published card pushes it right away.

Good to know

  • Only what's on your card goes online — your email is never published.
