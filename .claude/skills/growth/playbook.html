Tracks to contact

45 tracks and clubs from this week's research, nationwide. Every row links to the page it came from. Contact details aren't included because none were confirmed. Find the promoter on the track's Facebook page. Checkboxes are saved in this browser only.

Priority 1 · Kart and quarter midget clubs

Volunteer-run, scored by parents, lots of Junior classes and pill draws. A $200 packet is easy to approve, and they're the least likely to already be locked into a platform.

Priority 2 · Small weekly dirt and micro tracks

Weekly cards with several classes and points. Many use MyRacePass already, so pitch “keep your MRP site, run race night on RaceDay”.

Priority 3 · Bigger NASCAR-sanctioned asphalt

Established systems and staff. Worth one message each, mostly to learn what they use.
