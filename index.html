At the gate

Less time at the sign-up table.

At the sign-up table, type the driver's name into RaceDay on your check-in device — or tap it if they've raced with you before — and pick their classes. Their heat pill draws automatically, usually in under 15 seconds. You're still collecting waivers and entry fees at the table — RaceDay just skips the handwritten roster.

Heats build themselves the moment sign-up closes: pill order, inside/outside lines, B-mains and features, all laid out and ready to post before the drivers' meeting.

Plug any TV into the wall. RaceDay fills the screen with rotating lineups, laps, tonight's schedule and track updates — no crowding the tower to read a printout.
After the checkers

Enter finishes. Points update themselves.

Tap the cars in the order they finish — or use the number pad, or import a CSV straight from your timing system. Standings, the B-main, and the feature lineup all build from those numbers automatically.

Season points update the instant a feature is scored. No Monday-morning spreadsheet, no re-adding a season by hand.

Everything else

Organized, not just fast.

A driver book that remembers every racer year to year. Backups you can export anytime. A full audit trail of every result edit. Race-day history you can pull up months later.

Runs in a browser on whatever laptop or tablet you already own — nothing to install, nothing to configure on a server.

For everyone following the track

Every phone knows what's next.
